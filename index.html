Co-Annotator: Expert-Distilled ViT and VLM for Visual and Documentation Guidance in Age-Related Macular Degeneration

Ziheng “Leo” Li* · Benjamin Freeman* · Akshay Raman* · Kavin Aravindhan Rajkumar · Xinxin Fang · Rishabh Srivastava · Steven Feiner · Kaveri A. Thakoor

Columbia University

Reading a retinal OCT scan means knowing where to look and what to write. Co-Annotator distills both from retina specialists: their gaze trains a Vision Transformer that highlights areas of interest, and their dictation trains an ontology-bounded vision–language model that pre-fills an editable biomarker draft. Deployed with ophthalmology residents across two institutions, the combined guidance raised correct diagnoses per minute by 40% and cut comment editing time by 67%, without compromising diagnostic accuracy.

Paper arXiv 10.1145/3830398.3830722 Expert Corpus Biomarker VLM Gaze-aligned ViT

* co-first authors.

Overview

Highlights

  • Where to look: a Vision Transformer aligned to retina specialists' gaze shows toggleable area-of-interest heatmaps.
  • What to write: an ontology-bounded VLM, fine-tuned on expert dictation, pre-fills an editable biomarker draft.
  • Three user studies: expert data collection (US1), each modality in isolation (US2), and a combined deployment across two academic institutions (US3).
  • Design principles: clinical AI guidance should be deferrable, sparse, and evidence-anchored.
  • Open artifacts: the expert distillation corpus, the biomarker VLM, and the gaze-aligned ViT are on Hugging Face.

Key results

Combined guidance (US3)

With both modalities together, correct diagnoses per minute rose 40% (3.40 → 4.76, p = 0.023) and comment editing time fell 67% (7.2 s → 2.4 s, p = 0.023), with diagnostic accuracy preserved. Residents documented 5.36 biomarkers per AMD eye and kept 85.5% of the draft.

Each modality (US2)

Both modalities preserved residents' high baseline accuracy. Attention guidance left a post-guidance carryover: time per eye fell 28% after the heatmap was removed (p < 0.001). VLM drafts more than doubled biomarker documentation (2.3 → 5.8 per AMD eye), with 83.1% of the draft retained.

Expert distillation (US1)

Aligning the ViT to expert gaze improved diagnostic micro-AUC from 0.95 to 0.98. Fine-tuning the VLM on expert dictation brought its biomarker text close to the experts' own (MedBERTScore 0.867).
